
AI (Artificial Intelligence) is a branch of computer science which aims to develop intelligent systems, that is, systems that perform tasks humans normally do. 
They're systems that can learn, think, make decisions, research...

Some advantages are:
- AI allows to do repetitive /ripetitif/ tasks that we don't want to do. For example, I usually use AI at work when I need a piece of code that I know how to write but I don't feel like typing. I can ask it to do it and it does it for me. 
- AI allows us to get fast answers without needing to look for them on Google. 
- AI allows me to learn about a lot of topics I'm interested in like history, technology, science, travels, etc... 


Some disadvantages are: 
- In my opinion, AI could cause job losses and increase unemployment in the future  
- AI could make mistakes. This phenomenom is known as hallutinations. We can't expect that the AI results to be determinitics. AI results is obtained with a high influence of probability. 
- Energy consumption. Using AI requires an enormous energy consumption.
- Security problems. If we rely on AI for all our systems, I think that we could suffer security problems in the future.
