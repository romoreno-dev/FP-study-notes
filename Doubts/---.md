# PERFECT MODALS

## MUST HAVE
Deducción lógica sobre algo que ha sucedido en el pasado

- He didn't hear any voice. The thief must have gone. 
- The kitchen was clean. They must have gone to have lunch out. Blabla

## CAN'T HAVE
Deducción sobre algo que no pasó en el pasado basado en las evidencias del presente. 

- He can't have got the job. 
- She can't have passed such a difficult exam.

## MIGHT/MAY/COULD HAVE
Expresa posibilidad en el pasado

- He didn't even know how to program. They couldn't have given him the job.
- The little girl may have lost her keys
- Our neighbors might have heard some noises when our car was stolen

## COULD HAVE
Posibilidad en el pasado pero no se llevó a cabo. ahora es tarde.
- She could have called the doctor early in the morning.
- You could have been a good doctor, but you decided to study engineering. 

## SHOULD HAVE || OUGHT TO HAVE
Algo deseable en el pasado (opinión) pero ahora es tarde
Deber u obligación incumplido
- He should have taken some advice from his friend but he didn't no
- She should have adked you before borrowing your pen
-  Paul ought to have waited until the lights were green before he crossed the street, but he didn't wait. 
## SHOULDN'T HAVE
Arrepentimiento sobre hecho pasado. Tuvo lugar pero no era deseable.

- They shouldn't have gone out late last night, but they did.
- She shouldn't have taken the matter too seriously.

## NEEDN'T HAVE
Algo se hizo pero no era necesario. 
- When the party was over, I realized that you needn't have cooked so much food as most of it was untouched. 

## WOULD HAVE
En el Tercer condicional (Condición irreal del pasado que habría determinado otra del pasado)
- If my parents had had more money, I would have gone to university.
Y en el **mixto 2+3** (Condición irreal del presente que determina una del pasado)
- If I were rich, I would have bought that expensive car. 

### 2º Condicional
(Condición irreal del presente que determina una del presente)
- If I were rich, I would spend my life travelling
Y en el **mixto 3 + 2** (Condición irreal del pasado que determina una del presente)
- If I had studied harder, I would find a good job. (now)

- I wish I lived here
- Do you ever wish you could fly?

💡 **Diferencia con “used to” o “would”:**

1. **Past simple:** “We had breakfast together every morning.”
    
    - Simplemente relata lo que **pasaba en el pasado**, puede ser un hecho o rutina.
        
2. **Used to:** “We used to have breakfast together every morning.”
    
    - Enfatiza que **ya no lo hacemos**, hábito pasado.
        
3. **Would:** “We would have breakfast together every morning.”
    
    - También indica hábito pasado, más literario o narrativo.
        

En resumen:

- **Past simple** → rutina pasada sin comentar si sigue o no.
    
- **Used to / would** → hábito pasado que ya no ocurre (o narrativo).
