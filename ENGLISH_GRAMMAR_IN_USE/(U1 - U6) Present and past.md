
# 1. Present continuous 

- Hechos sucedidos en el momento en el que se habla(at the time fo speaking) (I'm trying to work)
- Hechos que comenzaron y aún no han terminado (I'm reading a really good book...)
- Cambio que está comenzando a pasar (**getting, becoming, starting, beginning, changing, improving, increasing, rising, falling, growing**). (Is your English getting better?; The population of the world is increasing very fast; At first I didn't like my job, but I'm starting to enjoy it now) 


Puede usarse con: today, this day, this year... (periods around now)
