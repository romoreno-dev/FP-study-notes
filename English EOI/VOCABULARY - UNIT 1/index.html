    Making decisions

    Pensar y reflexionar 🤔

    • To weigh up [posessive] options → ⚖️ Sopesar las opciones antes de decidir
    • To give it a lot of thoughts → 💭 Darle muchas vueltas a algo, reflexionar
    • To take into consideration → 📝 Tener en consideración, reflexionar
    • To be in two minds about something → 🤷‍♂️ No estar seguro entre dos opciones

    Tomar decisiones / decidirse ✅

    • To make up [posessive] mind → ✔️ Tomar una decisión
    • To make a rush decision → ⏩ Tomar una decisión apresurada y sin pensar
    • To change one's mind → 🔄 Cambiar de opinión
    • To have second thoughts → 🤔💭 Tener dudas después de decidir algo

    Seguir la intuición / corazonadas 🌟

    • To have a hunch → 🔮 Tener una corazonada
    • To go with one's gut feelings → ❤️ Seguir el presentimiento

    Descubrir / identificar problemas 🕵️‍♂️

    • To put a finger on something → 🖐️ Descubrir el origen de un problema / identificar la causa exacta de algo malo

    Mantener opciones abiertas 🔓

    • To keep one's options open → 🚪 Dejar la puerta abierta a más posibilidades

    She weighed up her options before choosing a university.
    He weighed up his options before deciding to move abroad.
    We need to weigh up all the options before making a final decision.

    I gave it a lot of thoughts before replying to her message.
    He gave it a lot of thoughts before quitting his job.
    She gave it a lot of thoughts and finally decided to travel alone.

    You should take into consideration the costs before buying a new car.
    We took into consideration all possible risks before starting the project.
    She took into consideration her friends’ advice before making a decision.

    I’m in two minds about moving to a new city.
    She was in two minds about accepting the job offer.
    He’s in two minds about buying a new phone.

    I can’t make up my mind which dress to buy.
    She finally made up her mind and accepted the offer.
    He made up his mind to start exercising every morning.

    Don’t make a rush decision; think it through.
    He made a rush decision and bought the first car he saw.
    They made a rush decision to move without checking the rent.

    I was going to go out, but I changed my mind.
    She changed her mind about moving abroad.
    He changed his mind after talking with his friends.

    I’m having second thoughts about quitting my job.
    She had second thoughts after signing the contract.
    They had second thoughts about moving to another country.

    I have a hunch that it’s going to rain today.
    She had a hunch something was wrong.
    He had a hunch the team would win the match.

    I went with my gut feelings and chose the red dress.
    She went with her gut feelings and trusted him.
    He went with his gut feelings and invested in the company.

    I can’t put my finger on what’s causing the problem.
    She finally put her finger on the reason for the delay.
    He put his finger on the issue that everyone had missed.

    I’m keeping my options open until I get a better offer.
    She decided to keep her options open before committing.
    He’s keeping his options open in case something better comes along.

    quite, pretty, rather and fairly

    quite y pretty son menos que "very" pero más que "a little"

    quite: Va antes de a/an + adj. pretty: Va después de a/an + adj y se usa en Inglés informal

    Sarah has quite a good job. Sarah 
Sarah has a pretty good job.

    quite, quite a lot of puede usarse también sin adjetivo, solo con un nombre - It was quite a surprise (big surprise) - There were quite a lot of guests at the wedding

    quite + V - I quite like tennis, but it's not my favourite sport. (Me gusta bastante el tennis)

    rather Igual que quite pero se usa para ideas negativas. - It's rather cloudy - Paul is rather shy. O ideas positivas pero sorpresivas - These orange are rather good. Where did you get them?

    Fairly Más débil que quite/rather/pretty. "Fairly good", no es muy bueno podría ser mejor - We see each other faily often, but not as often as we used to. - My room is failr big, but I'd preffer a bigger one.

    Quite tiene sentido de "completamente" con adjetivos como sure, right, true, clear, diffferent, incredible, amazing, certaing, wrong, safe, obvious, unnecesarry, extraordinary, impossible. - She was quite different from what I expected. - Everything they said was quite true - I quite sure - It's quite right

    Not quite (no completamente) - I don't quite understand what you mean - Are you ready yet? Not quire

    • The story is quite interesting (menos que "muy")
    • The story is quite true (completamente cierta)

    I pretty much agree with you (casi completamente de acuerdo contigo) I've pretty much finished my homework (casi he terminado mi tarea) It's pretty much the same thing (son casi la misma cosa) I know pretty much what you mean (mas o menos entiendo lo que quieres decir) I know pretty much everything about computers (sé casi todo sobre computadores)
