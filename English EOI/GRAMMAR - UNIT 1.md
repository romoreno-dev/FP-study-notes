
## Question formation

There are two types of questions:

#### Yes-No Question

- Se responden con patrón de sí-no
- Siguen la estructura:   **Auxiliary Verb / Modal Verb** + **Sujeto** + **Main Verb** ?
- Se responden con short answers.


| Question                  | Short Answer                           | Long Answer                                                   |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Are you studying English? | Yes, I’m \| No, I’m not                | Yes, I am studying English \| No, I am not studying English   |
| Is she happy?             | Yes, she’s \| No, she isn’t            | Yes, she is happy \| No, she is not happy                     |
| Do you like pizza?        | Yes, I do \| No, I don’t               | Yes, I like pizza \| No, I don’t like pizza                   |
| Did he call you?          | Yes, he did \| No, he didn’t           | Yes, he called me \| No, he didn’t call me                    |
| Will they come?           | Yes, they will \| No, they won’t       | Yes, they will come \| No, they will not come                 |
| Can she swim?             | Yes, she can \| No, she can’t          | Yes, she can swim \| No, she can’t swim                       |
| Should they study more?   | Yes, they should \| No, they shouldn’t | Yes, they should study more \| No, they shouldn’t study more  |
| Might he come tomorrow?   | Yes, he might \| No, he might not      | Yes, he might come tomorrow \| No, he might not come tomorrow |


#### Wh-Questions

- Contienen una **question word** (When, Where, Who, Why, What, How, How much-many, How often)
- Siguen la estructura: **Question Word** + **Auxiliary Verb / Modal Verb** + **Suj** + **Main verb** ?

- Se pueden usar **negative questions** para **expresar sorpresa** ante algo inesperado  o para **verificar** algo que se cree cierto. 

		Examples:
		- Can't you swim? Yes, I can (Sí, puede nadar)
		- Aren't you tired? Yes, I am  (Sí, estoy cansado)
		- Haven't you done your homework? Yes, I have (Sí, la he hecho)
		- Isn't it amazing? Yes, I it is (Sí, lo es)
		- Isn't this a beautiful place? Yes, it is (Sí, lo es)

- Si el verbo viene normalmente seguido de una preposición, la preposición va **al final de la oración**.
		- What are you thinking about? 
		- Who does this bag belong to?
  A menudo solo se usa la **question word** + **preposición**
		- What about?

```
A: I’m worried about something.  
B (larga): What are you thinking about?  
B (corta): About what?

A: I saw her yesterday, and she looked strange.  
B (larga): Who were you looking at?  
B (corta): Who at?

A: I was talking to someone on the phone.  
B (larga): Who were you talking to?  
B (corta): Who to?

A: I’ve been standing here for ages.  
B (larga): Who are you waiting for?  
B (corta): Who for?

A: I don’t really agree with him.  
B (larga): Who do you agree with?  
B (corta): Who with?

A: It depends on the weather.  
B (larga): What does it depend on?  
B (corta): What on?
```

- Cuando la **question word es el sujeto de la pregunta** en **present o past simple** no se usa do/did.
		> En definitiva, si vamos a responder con un sujeto, no se pone. 

	Example: **Who wrotes Shakespeare?**
- William Shakespeare wrote him.  ← formal y completa
- William Shakespeare.  ← suficiente en conversación

	Example: **Who lives in that house?**
-  Sheriff Wiggum 
- Sheriff Wiggum lives there

```
WHO
Who called you yesterday?  
→ John did.  ← “Who” es el sujeto
Who did you call yesterday?  
→ I called John.  ← “Who” es el objeto

WHAT
What made that noise?  
→ The wind did.  ← “What” es el sujeto
What did you make yesterday?  
→ I made a cake.  ← “What” es el objeto

WHICH
Which of these books belongs to you?  
→ This one does.  ← “Which” es el sujeto
Which book did you buy?  
→ I bought this one.  ← “Which” es el objeto

WHOSE
Whose idea won the contest?  
→ Maria’s did.  ← “Whose idea” es el sujeto
Whose book did you borrow?  
→ I borrowed John’s.  ← “Whose book” es el objeto

WHERE
Where is happening the meeting?  ← Muy formal / raro  
Where did you go yesterday?  
→ I went to the park.  ← “Where” es el objeto de “go”

WHEN, WHY, HOW
When did you arrive?  
→ I arrived at 8.  ← “When” no es sujeto
Why did she leave early?  
→ She left because she was tired.  ← “Why” no es sujeto
How did you solve the problem?  
→ I solved it with patience.  ← “How” no es sujeto
```


## Indirect questions

- Las **indirect questions** se usan para preguntar de una forma más educada 
- Siguen el orden **sujeto** + **verbo** y no se usa do-does/did en la segunda parte de la pregunta. Se puede usar if/wether si es una yes-no question.
