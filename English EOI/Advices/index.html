    Advices

    En inglés, cuando una palabra funciona como modificador (por ejemplo, un sustantivo que describe a otro sustantivo), normalmente se usa en singular, aunque se hable de algo plural.

    Por eso se dice: - question formation (formación de preguntas) - car park (estacionamiento de coches, no cars park) - shoe store (tienda de zapatos, no shoes store) - toothbrush (cepillo de dientes, no teethbrush)

    Manner / Place / Time

    Preposiciones lugar: In vs To

    “to” → dirección / movimiento hacia un lugar - Se usa cuando hablamos de ir a un lugar, sin implicar necesariamente que permanecemos allí.

    “in” → dentro de un lugar, indicando ubicación / residencia / permanencia - Se usa cuando algo o alguien está dentro de un lugar, es decir, se encuentra allí. Se centra en estar en un sitio.

    I haven't lived in Toledo. I only went to Toledo for a holiday last year.

    Preposiciones medios de transporte: In, On, By

    Usar by para todo lo genérico, salvo "on feet". Y usar on para todo lo específico (artículo, posesivo) salvo in para coches. y si se usa take / catch uno puede ahorrarse la preposición.

    For vs To

    "To" primarily indicates: - Purpose or intention (propósito o intención). Con to-. Finalidad concreta de la acción específica. - I study hard to pass the exam.
    - Direction or movement toward a destination (dirección / movimiento hacia un lugar)
    - We are going to the park.
    - The recipient of an action (destinatario de una acción) - Giving - I gave the book to my friend. - A range or limit (rango o límite) - The temperature rose to 30°C. - It's a quarter to two - Comparing - I prefer playing football to working

    "For" primarily indicates: - Purpose or function (propósito o función). Con -ing. Propósito general. Función o uso de algo. - We stopped for petrol. - Benefit or advantage (beneficio / ventaja) - Exercise is good for your health. - Duration or period of time (duración o período de tiempo) - I have lived here for five years. - Representation or support (representación o apoyo) - The union provides support for its members. - Schedule - I made an appointment for April 2026 - Agree with - Are you for or against this situation?

    "Para..."

    For + Noun To + Verb
    - We stopped for petrol
    - I had to run for the bus    		 - We stopped to get petrol
    - I had to run to catch the bus

    To (To + verb) - Indica la finalidad concreta de algo - Do you have much work to do? - It's hard to find a place to park in the city centre - Would you like something to eat - Puede tener una preposición después del verbo - I need something to open this bottle with

    for somebody to do something (para alguien para hacer algo) - There weren't any chairs for us to sit on, so we sat on the floow.

    for (do)ing (para dar definición general, decir que algo se usa para) - This brush is for washing the dishes

    Para destacar la finalidad usar to-. Nunca se usa for -ing en ese caso - I went into the kitchen to wash the dishes

    Usar What ...for para preguntar por el propósito - What is this switch for? - What did you do that for?

    so that so Se usa so that (no se usa to) con can/could y will/would

    • She's learning English so that she can study in Canada
    • We moved to London so that we could see our friends more often
    • I hurried so that I wouldn't be late

    También se puede omitir el that. - I hurried so I wouldn't be late.

    enough/too for y enough/too to

    Recuerda que el orden es: too + adj y adj + enough

    Se usa for para alguien/alguna cosa y to para hacer algo - Thi bag isn't big enought for all my clotes - That shirt is too small for you - She's not enough to have a driving license - The bridge is** just wide enough** for two cars to pass one another.

    Just

    Expresa que fue hace poco. "Acabar de" - I' ve just had lunch - Have you just arrived? - I'd just had lunch.

    (American English: Hace uso del past simple para acontecimientos recientes en vez del past perfect) - I just had lunch. - Did you just arrived?

    Just in case: Por si acaso. Takes an umbrella, just in case it rains. Just in time: Justo a tiempo. I arrived at the meeting just on time. (Justo a tiempo para llegar tarde)

    Just as: Exactamente en ese momento. Just as I sat down, the doorbell rang.

    On time vs in time

    • On time. Puntual a tiempo
    • In time. Justo antes de que suceda algo.
